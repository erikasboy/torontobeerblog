Hot damn! After a busy day a little Nerve Tonic from @eastboundbeer is just the ticket. Beautiful snappy saison, with lovely yeasty esters. The juniper is present but delicately so. I probably wouldn’t have minded if it was a gin-bomb, but I really like this. Worth also mentioning the fabulous grain bill. It’s sweet, with a little caramel goodness, and bit of brown bread. Just lovely beer .
