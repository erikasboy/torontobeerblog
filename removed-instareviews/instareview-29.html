The other day I made a rare trip to the @lcbo and decided to grab cans of #ipa that I don’t normally reach for, either out of habit or because they’re not available at Loblaws. White’s Bay IPA from @thecollingwoodbrewery is one of the former. But by golly it’s a good one. Beautiful hazy orange, with lots of citrus and pine in the hops, and an assertive bitterness. Great weighty and smooth body, and a lingering hoppy finish with just a little citrus tang. Rock solid .
