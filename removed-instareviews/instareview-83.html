An evening in playing cards with the fam and sipping on this banger from @sawdustcitybeer. Electric Storm is no just a favourite U2 song (with an “al”) but a lovely sour oat IPA, which is tart without being biting. It’s creamy smooth, with a pleasingly dry hoppiness. And it’s got a guitar effect pedal on the can so, win! Bigups to @samcorbeil and the whole crew up at Sawdust. Lovely stuff! .
