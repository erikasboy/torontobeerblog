Woah, @mybrewingco Jail Fire Rauchbier is an amazingly accurate example of the style. Definitely not for everybody, but it shows those classic rich beechwood-smoked malts, and a clean yeast profile. I’d say Jail Fire hits more on the bbq part of the smoke-flavour-line (not as ashy as “campfire” and not as rich as “bacon”). This really drinks like one of those smoky lagers from Bamberg. Grab some at the brewery now! .
