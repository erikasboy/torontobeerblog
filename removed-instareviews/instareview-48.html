Good golly this Loral Lager from @greatlakesbeer is tasty. It tastes like the hops East Kent Goldings and Cascade had a baby. Snappy and bitter, initially with a nice herbal quality that finishes slightly fruity/citrusy. The Malta are nicely cracker-like, and the finish is quick, dry, with jus a little orange juice. As it warmed up a little some floral notes started to come through as well, especially on the finish. Fabulous stuff, glad I grabbed some!
