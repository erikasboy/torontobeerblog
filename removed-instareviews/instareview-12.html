I have a confession about this #IPA from @andersoncraftales: literally every time I buy beer at Loblaws, I wander down the beer aisle, grab my usual assortment of Mountain Lager, Canuck, Boneshaker, and Ransack, and continue along. Then I pass the end-aisle display and see the lovely minimalist packaging from Anderson, and am tempted to grab a 6, but I don’t want to put anything back, so I just keep walking. Today, though, I came at the aisle from the other direction, and immediately grabbed a six and am SO glad I did. What a fantastic IPA! Bright hops with floral notes and a lovely shot of melons. Smooth rich body, and a clean hoppy finish, this is bolts! Don’t be like me, but this often (as I will now)!
