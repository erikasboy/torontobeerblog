Dessert after an absolute banger of a dinner is this interesting lacto IPA from @sawdustcitybeer. Boa Vida is, admittedly, not in my usual wheelhouse. I get down to dank or fruity or citrusy or tropical IPAs just fine, but I confess that I tend to pass on lacto or vanilla or the word “milkshake”. That said, this is pretty nice. The aroma is fruity and tropical, with a clear grainy presence. It tastes mainly of pineapple and citrus fruit, with the lacto adding weight and some creamy sweetness, without being overly milky. There’s a very peachy quality to the finish, and while I probably wouldn’t drink multiple in one evening, this single can is delightful. It ticks some trendy/hip boxes without screaming about it from the rooftop .
