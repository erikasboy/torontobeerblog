Chris Schryer is a Toronto-based beer writer and educator. He has appeared on TV, radio and print, discussing various aspects of beer culture, from reviews and news, to brewing history and technique, to the politics of beer locally and abroad. Please feel free to contact him using the form here. Head-shots for news or appearances can be downloaded here (in a .zip archive with web and high-resolution images).
