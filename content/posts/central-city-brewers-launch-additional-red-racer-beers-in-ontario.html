Introduces Red Racer India Session Ale (ISA), Red Racer Copper Ale & Reign of Hops Mixer Pack

Surrey, B.C. – The wait is finally over for Ontario craft beer fans who have been eagerly anticipating the arrival BC-based Central City Brewers + Distillers’ award-winning craft beers. The brewer is pleased to announce their expansion in Ontario with their latest additions, Red Racer India Session Ale (ISA), Red Racer Copper Ale, and the Reign of Hops 12-can Mixer Pack. These products join their best selling Red Racer IPA, which is already available at various LCBO stores throughout the province.

[caption id="attachment_4394" align="alignright" width="165"] Red Racer ISA — Central City Brewers + Distillers[/caption]

Central City had previously announced their expansion strategy into Eastern Canada with the appointment of James Hume, Director of Sales. The brewer has now added additional salespeople working with James.

“There is no shortage of demand for our award-winning craft beers especially in Ontario where the craft beer market is growing rapidly,” says Tim Barnes, Vice President of Sales and Marketing at Central City. “We continue to invest in our expansion into the Eastern provinces, and now have a robust team in place to bring more of our products to beer fans in Ontario.”

The Red Racer India Session Ale is a light, crisp, and fruity ale with a good bitterness and malt balance, coupled with refreshing hop aromatics. The use of mosaic hops gives this full-flavoured craft beer exotic tropical fruit notes with hints of passion fruit. The craft ale is full on flavour but light on alcohol, with only four per cent ABV, which is perfect for those with active lifestyles. The ISA is best enjoyed when paired with lighter foods like pizza or seafood. The ISA will soon be available in 473 ml cans at LCBO stores throughout Ontario.

The Red Racer Copper Ale is a light bodied, easy-drinking, American-style amber ale with a pleasantly dry finish. It is made with toasted Munich malt, which gives this beer both its reddish, amber colour and malty flavour, and is blended with Caravienne and Caramunich malts. The Ale is subtly balanced with German Perle and Amarillo hops and is nicely paired with BBQ and pork dishes, fruit salad, and even caramel pecan pie. The Copper Ale is soon available in both 6-pack cans, and 500ml single cans at select Beer Store locations in Ontario.

The Central City Reign of Hops Mixer Pack features Red Racer ISA, Red Racer IPA and Red Racer Pale Ale. Reign of Hops will be available in 12-pack cans at select Beer Store locations in Ontario.

About Central City Brewers + Distillers:

Central City is a craft brewery in the business of developing, producing and selling international award-winning premium craft beer and high quality distilled spirits brewed without compromise. Based in Surrey, British Columbia, CCBD was voted Canada’s Best Brewery of the Year in 2010 and 2012. Its Red Racer brand has become a cult icon among craft beer enthusiasts throughout North America. Founded in 2003 as a brewpub and liquor store in downtown Surrey, CCBD celebrated its 10th anniversary in 2013 with the grand opening of a brand new, 68,000 sq. ft. brewery and distillery on Bridgeview Drive. www.centralcitybrewing.com
