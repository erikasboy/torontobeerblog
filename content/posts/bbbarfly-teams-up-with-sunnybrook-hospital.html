The coolest bottle opener just got a little more cool.  I got this note a little while back from Brian Becker, co-owner of BBbarfly.  I love seeing companies team up with charitable organizations; well done BBbarfly!

Thinking about beer? Think about your brain and donating to Sunnybrook Hospital stroke research, says BBbarfly.

BBbarfly, makers of the world’s best bottle opener, is teaming with Toronto’s Festival of Beer to help make a difference for Sunnybrook.

Each sale of the unique butterfly-knife inspired opener will send $2 Sunnybrook’s way and help grow vital stroke research. BBbarfly will also be accepting donations from beer fans at the Toronto’s Festival of Beer #27 booth at the event, August 4 to 7.

“Stroke is the third leading cause of death in Canada,” said Brian Becker, BBbarfly owner. “We’re honored to help Sunnybrook in this good cause and want to thank the Toronto’s Festival of Beer for their support.”

Toronto’s Festival of Beer will attract 40,000 beer lovers who will have a chance to taste some of the 120 brew varieties from around the world. BBbarfly’s quality state-of-the-art bottle opener, designed to market products by blazing branding across its handles, is a featured product at the Toronto’s Festival of Beer.

More information can be found online at http://www.bbbarfly.com/
