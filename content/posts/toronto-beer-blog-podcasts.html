Well, it's official.  I'm actually trying to start out my weekly podcasts! If you remember from my January 5th resolutions, I resolved to start a podcast.  I do a weekly bit on the Edge with Fearless Fred, which I love and is great, but has some structural limitations.  I can't really rant on and on, and basically people have to come into the studio to talk.  Using Google Hangouts, I am able to reach out to anybody, anywhere, as long as they have a webcam and an internet connection. I'm also able to talk about anything and let the conversation go where ever it may. So if you're interested in beer, watch here, or better, subscribe to the podcast once it is live (apparently it takes iTunes a while to archive it).  There will be both a video feed, and an audio file.
