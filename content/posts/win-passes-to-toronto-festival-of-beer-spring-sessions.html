We Have a Winner!

Congratulations to Grace Kelly, enjoy the fest!

Well hello Toronto (and surrounding area) (and, well, the rest of the world, but unless you're traveling here this Friday, this maybe won't mean much to you). Do you like beer? Kind of silly question. You're reading a blog this very instant, that is entirely about beer. Do you want to drink beer with large crowds of like-minded folks? Well who doesn't? (if you don't, again, go ahead and tune out now). Well have I got news for you!

I've got a pair of passes to give-away, for this Friday's Toronto Festival of Beer Spring Sessions!

It goes down at Sherbourne Common, this Friday from 4-10pm. There's going to be a heap of great beer, amazing food, and music from Lowell! You can dig all the details here: http://beerfestival.ca/spring/

So, want a pair of passes? There's a bit of a challenge: tell us what brewery you're stoked to check out. Use Twitter, Instagram, and/or Facebook.

A tweet might look like this: "I can't wait to drink @sawdustcitybeer at @TOBeerFestival Spring Session, @ChrisSchryer! #BeerMe". An Instagram entry could look the same, but with a picture too. Same with a Facebook post. All entries will be pooled, and a winner will be randomly selected (using the good old excel "randbetween"). Want more than one entry? Okay, here's how you get extra chances to win:

  • Send a tweet, update, or gram, and tag me in it 1 entry (@ChrisSchryer twitter/instagram, facebook.com/chris.schryer or facebook.com/torontobeerblog)
  • Tag Toronto's Festival of Beer in the update 1 entry
  • Tag the brewery you're referencing 1 entry
  • Use the #BeerMe hashtag 1 entry
  • Include a photo 3 entries
  • Link to a review from TorontoBeerBlog.com of a beer from the brewery you're tagging 5 entries
  • All of those looks like 12 entries, right? Except if you do them all in a single post add 3 entries for a total of 15
  • You can do a post on each platform, and collect entries from each one, so you could earn 45 entries. Remember, an "@" tagged post from twitter doesn't tag properly in Facebook, you'll need to make that post separately. The entries only get awarded if the people in question are properly tagged.

You can tell me about being excited to try something once on each platform. If you tell me more than once on a single platform, I will ignore them all.

All statuses, tweets, and grams must be sent by 11:59pm EDT Thursday, April 23rd, 2015.

All good? Let's do this. Check out the Festival page and tell me who you're excited to sample, send me a pic I'll like, tag me, the fest, the brewer, and find a solid link to share. Go!
