A new day, and a new batch of four Sunday tickets for Toronto Festival of Beer! In addition to the excellent beers and food, this year we will be entertained by Easy Star Allstars and Big Sugar!  Today's tickets can be yours, if you answer this simple question: What band plays Thursday nights at Castro's Lounge? All correct entries will be pooled and the winner will be drawn from them.

Please note, you will need to come to the east end of Toronto (or have a friend do so) to pick up the tickets.  Contest closes at midnight EST tonight (Tuesday, July 23).

*** This contest is now closed. Congratulations to today’s winner. For more contests, check the contest page. ***
