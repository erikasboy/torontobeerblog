Dark Star is an English brewery, and according to their label, they've won some nice awards.  I first had this beer at the LCBO fall release tasting, and really enjoyed it.  I picked up a bottle to review properly, and it's been sitting waiting for me since.  I was really taken by the label, which has this chunky design to it.  And the beer was pretty good too.  Let's see if I still like it.

[caption id="attachment_969" align="aligncenter" width="400" caption="Espresso Stout - Dark Star Brewing Co."][/caption]

From a 500ml bottle with a great label and no freshness date I could find, the beer is an opaque black with a little bit of tan head.  head drops to a film that hangs around for the duration of the pint.  Aroma is really dark coffee, a little smoky and charred.  Hints of sweet malts and maybe rum, but the coffee is the main element.  Taste is again quite a bit of coffee, though now the sweet/rum flavours are brown sugar like.  Heaps of dry coffee bitterness on the finish.  Mouthfeel is very light with a nice carbonation and fairly clean dry finish.  As a stout I could probably drink it all night, though the coffee taste might slow me down a bit.  Great beer, shame it's probably all gone from the LCBO now.
