So, you might remember from my earlier post on TFOB, that I'm running a photographic scavenger hunt during the festival.  The deal is, take pictures of the stuff on the list below, upload them to Facebook, Flickr, Tumblr or whatever, and share them with me.  I'm looking for pictures that will make me smile; both humour and artistry are appreciated.  You do not need to submit a picture for every item on the list; quality over quantity.  Please make sure you title each picture you submit with what number it corresponds to, and add a creative title, if you would like.  Images can be taken anywhere inside bandshell park during the three days of Toronto's Festival of Beer or The Queer Beer Fest.  Please don't break any laws, either legal or ethical, in the capturing of these images.

There will be three top prizes, as well as some honourable mentions for specific "stuff".

Deadline for submissions is Friday, August 12th at 11:59pm EST. 

Things to snap (or "Pictures to Make" as the Dutch would say):

  1. Mud
  2. Best beer logo positioning
  3. Someone in wellies, drinking a Welly
  4. You and a brewmaster (points for how much I like the beer they make)
  5. Best BBbarfly martial-arts pose
  6. A celebrity
  7. Best TFOB goatee
  8. "What made Milwaukee famous, made a loser out of me"
  9. Best free thing you got given
  10. A beer nut
  11. The 5-0
  12. Something political
  13. Green beer
  14. A silly hat
  15. Impress me
  16. A bud girl, drinking an OCB beer (*must* be in branded cup; points for the relative merit of the bud girl)
  17. Something organic
  18. Something inorganic
  19. Two heads are better than one
  20. Lots of volume
