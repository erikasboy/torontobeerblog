That is not just a clever title.  This, indeed is a job posting for a Brewery Operations Manager, and the company is in the Bahamas.  Those geography nuts reading will know that Paradise is an island in the Bahamas.

Look at that picture.  Now picture Toronto in February.  Back to the picture; back to TO in Feb.  See?  This is your chance to take all your great Canadian brewing experience, and head to the sunny south.  Bikinis and rum and coconuts and summer 365.  While we're pushing our cars out of snowbanks to fight through horrible traffic where you're seconds away from sliding into parked vehicles, you will be walking to the brewery in flip-flops with sand in your toes.

Basically, you would have to be crazy not to at least entertain the possibility of applying. So what's the job description?  I'm glad you asked:

Operations Manager

Island Beverage Company Ltd.

Job Title: Operations Manager

Location: Caribbean

Posted On: Oct 20, 2011

Job Type: Full-Time

Experience Required: 5 yrs +

Job Summary: Seeking a Beverage Operations Manager with a minimum of 5 year’s experience in a supervisory role in a brewery, water or other beverage manufacturing plant.

Job Description

We are growing rapidly from a small microbrewery and water bottling company and we need someone that has been through this type of expansion before and knows how to take a microbrewery or water company to the next level. The position will report to the Company President and will be responsible for overseeing and managing all departments in the brewery including brewhouse, packaging, production planning, shipping/receiving, QC laboratory and maintenance. The desired candidate will have excellent organizational, planning and management skills, and must work well with others. The ideal candidate will have prior experience in all areas of brewery operations, or other beverage manufacturing operations.

If you want to live in the Caribbean and earn tax free income, you can get in on the ground floor of this rapidly growing company. This is a small island nation is the hidden jewel of the Caribbean and is also conveniently located to North America with direct flights daily to the USA and Canada. We operate as an open book company and all employees are knowledgeable about all aspects of the business. They all “wear many different hats” and all  managers and employees are expected to help out at all levels of the operations in all aspects of the business.

Essential Duties

Plan, organize and direct the manufacturing and maintenance operations which ensure the most effective return on assets. Manage spending against budget and in relation to changes in production volume.

Qualification

Dedicated and proven leader able to work hands-on and closely with all business functions. 5+ years’ experience in a high production microbrewery environment with minimum 2 years experience. Experience in water, juice or other beverage manufacturing would also be beneficial. High energy required. Independent thinking, strong organizational skills and planning abilities, as well as excellent analytical and problem solving skills are essential.

If you are ready to experience the excitement of living and working overseas, and like the idea of learning tax free income in the beautiful Caribbean, then: Send Resume to islandbeverages@gmail.com and someone will contact you.
