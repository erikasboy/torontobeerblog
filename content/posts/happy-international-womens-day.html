From the whole crew down here at TorontoBeerBlog.com, we'd like to wish everybody a big huge happy International Women's Day. I know not a small number of women who are huge superstars, but for the sake of this blog, I'd like to give special recognition to the wonderful women who make and sell beer around Toronto. Without much effort, I can think of at least a dozen solid females directly involved in the scene here. And that's saying something, in what is often perceived as a male-dominated industry. Hint, it's certainly got a lot of dudes in it, but to suggest they're dominant is ridiculous.
