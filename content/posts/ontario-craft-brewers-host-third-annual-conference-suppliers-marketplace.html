TORONTO (September 26, 2014) – Ontario Craft Brewers will host their third annual, one-day industry conference on Thursday, October 16, 2014 at the Allstream Centre (Exhibition Place), 105 Princes Blvd,
in Toronto. The Ontario Craft Brewers Conference & Suppliers Marketplace 2014 is the craft brewing industry’s ultimate day of education, celebration, and business-to-business connecting.

[caption id="attachment_4249" align="aligncenter" width="540"] OCB Conference will be in Toronto again this year.[/caption]

“The craft brewing industry is expanding worldwide,” says Sybil Taylor, Communications Director, Steam Whistle Brewing and Chair of Ontario Craft Brewers’ Conference Advisory Committee. “In the past decade, we have seen tremendous growth in craft brewing in Ontario and our conference has responded with an expanded agenda to address the opportunities and issues facing our craft brewing brethren. Our Suppliers Marketplace is 28% larger than last year and we hope to double our attendance from the first year.”

Speakers from Ontario Craft Breweries, industry experts and suppliers will address the 350+ attendees on a range of technical and professional topics including branding, marketing, brewing, packaging, HR, legal issues and ingredients. The conference learning sessions will conclude with the ever-popular ‘Pioneers Panel’, when all attendees will gather to hear of the trials and triumphs from long-standing legends of the Ontario Craft brewing world: Ron Keefe of Granite Brewery, Michael Hancock of Side Launch Brewing, and Charles Maclean, Maclean Ales. The hour long session will be moderated by Muskoka Brewery President and former OCB Chair, Gary McMullen.

“This will be Ontario Craft Brewers’ industry event of the year,” says Taylor. “Although we are all competitors in the craft beer marketplace, this conference shows how co-operative we are, with some of the newest entrants being helped along with advice from the pioneers. This conference focuses on quality; and, the entire industry, consumers included, wins, when our collective standards are raised.”
The conference is open to any organizations, individuals, breweries, brewers and associates from Canada and the U.S. who wish to attend. Tickets can be purchased online and the full agenda can be accessed on the conference website http://ontariocraftbrewers.com/conference/

Ontario Craft Brewers: Discover the Difference. The Ontario Craft Brewers is an association of 45+ small brewers dedicated to making great tasting, high quality beer in Ontario. Ontario Craft Brewers brew their beers locally using fresh, all natural ingredients in communities throughout Ontario - from the Ottawa Valley to Windsor and Niagara to Muskoka and as far north as Kenora. Handcrafting over 350 premium beers, Ontario Craft Brewers employ almost 1,000 people in Ontario, accounting for almost 30 per cent of the overall brewing employment in the province. Ontario's Craft Brewers' share for premium priced brands has more than tripled since 2002, going from less than one per cent to over three per cent of the beer volume sold in Ontario and continues to be the fastest growing segment within the LCBO's beer category. The OCB's long term vision is to make Ontario a North American Centre of Excellence for Craft Brewing. Funding provided by the government of Ontario. Please discover responsibly.
