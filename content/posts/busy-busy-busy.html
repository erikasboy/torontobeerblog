After a bit of time enjoying the fine Beau's products, I'll be hopping over to an event hosted by Hop City.  Seems to be an awareness party, as in, "this is who we are and what we do".  I get beer, food and swag, so I'm there.  By the way, they are now identifying themselves openly as a subsidiary of Moosehead.  I like the Olands, though not so much most of their brews.  That being said, I found Hop City's lager to be an easy-going drink.  Not super-complex, but quite a bit better then Moosehead Lager.
