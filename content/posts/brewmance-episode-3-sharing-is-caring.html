Well, last month's Brewmance wasn't a home run, but this month, we've got Iain McOustra from Amsterdam Brewing Co. and a big old jug of Brettanomyces Bruxellensis.  Indeed, we're going with a 100% brett pale ale, and a huge shot of fruity hops to finish it.  Full video below, and recipe (sort of) there after.

Sharing is Caring 100% Brett Pale Ale

For a 5 gallon batch

2.5kg 2-Row (we actually used a Maris blend)
2.5kg Vienna Malt
6g Columbus Hops
2oz Mosaic Hops
2oz Galaxy Hops
1 tablet Whirlfloc
Brettanomyces Brux

Mash at 65C for 1 hour. Boil 60 minutes. 60 minute hop addition: 6g Columbus. 5 minute hop addition 1oz Galaxy 1oz Mosaic. Flame out hop addition 1oz Galaxy 1oz Mosaic. 10 minute whirlpool, 10 minute rest. Cool to 21C and pitch yeast. Ferment at 23C.

SG 1.058
16 IBU
