Another day, and more TFOB tickets! Today is looking like a beautiful day, so show me something beautiful and beery, for your chance to win four passes to the Sunday Session, featuring Easy Star Allstars and Big Sugar.  Either on Facebook, Twitter or Instagram, post a beer picture and tag me in it (facebook.com/chris.schryer, Twitter/Instagram @ChrisSchryer).  Best pic wins (as totally and completely subjectively judged by me).  All entries must be posted by midnight tonight.
