Steam Whistle Brewing brings Oktoberfest’s authentic experience to Canada and invites beer lovers from across the country to clank their steins and celebrate the largest festival in the world. Steam Whistle will be celebrating Oktoberfest with the Ultimate Oktoberfest Party on Sept 20th at the brewery.

[caption id="attachment_4180" align="aligncenter" width="600"] Steam Whistle a-plenty[/caption]

“ After taking our staff on a trip to tour Czech breweries and attending the world-famous Oktoberfest, we knew we had to bring part of the rich brewing culture home and recreate the magic here at Steam Whistle,” explains brewery co-founder Cam Heaps. “This is our 4th year celebrating Oktoberfest and it’s more than just a party; it’s about bringing the camaraderie, song, food, folk culture and pageantry that Germany is renowned for right here to Ontario. And we invite beer lovers to join in Steam Whistle’s take on this renowned beer celebration.”

Ultimate Oktoberfest party at the Brewery

Get your oom-pah-pah on and join our traditional Oktoberfest Party on Saturday, September 20th from 2p.m.to 11p.m. at the Brewery! Enjoy authentic German food, folk music and entertainment while you hoist steins of Steam Whistle Pilsner for revelry and refreshment. Just like the special Munich Oktoberfest ritual, our Brewmaster Marek will be tapping the first keg to signal the beginning of the festivities. Tickets are $30 at the door and include a souvenir stein (to take home) and its first beer fill on us. Come dressed in costume and our staff will too! Read our 7 easy tips to dress Oktoberfestive here. Parties will be taking place across the county. Visit www.steamwhistle.ca/oktoberfest

The Steins

Order a liter stein of Steam Whistle Pilsner and KEEP THE STEIN! Bars across Ontario are joining the  celebration by offering the Steam Whistle Oktoberfest ceramic steins from September 8th through October, while quantities last. Some bars are featuring a “Stein Re-fill” option. We will be posting full listing on our website.

Now Available! Oktoberfest Gift Pack with Ceramic Stein

A true Oktoberfest experience needs a stein. Traditionally, beer maids must be able to carry 10 one-liter mugs at a time! The record number of liter steins carried at once was 19! Five in each hand and nine on top, by Bavarian waitress, Anita Schwarz. You can start practicing by picking up the Oktoberfest Gift Pack which includes 2 x 500ml cans of Steam Whistle Pilsner and a 1 liter ceramic Oktoberfest stein decorated with traditional beer festival scenes. Gift packs now available at your local LCBO while quantities last. ($15.45 each)
