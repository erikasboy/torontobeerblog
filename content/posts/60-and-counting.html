Nearly days after James from BrewDog confirmed the sale of 12 bottles of 55% beer, called "The End of History", packaged in stuffed dead animals, there's a new biggest beer:  "Start the Future", from Holland's 't Koelschip (The Refrigerated Ship) brewery.  A little more accessable to the average drinker (The End of History cost 500 pounds sterling), "Start the Future" is $35 Euro for a 330ml bottle.  Interestingly enough, the article doesn't mention taste.....
