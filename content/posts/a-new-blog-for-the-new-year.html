Nearly two years ago to the day, I started Toronto Beer Blog, not so much as a resolution, but as a New Year project.  Because you're reading this, I would have to say it's enjoyed some success.  And so I'm super excited to see another blogger enter the fray as a New Year's resolution.  A beer-minded fellow named Greg in New Brunswick has started up the Atlantic Canada Beer Blog, and certainly has a great market to cover.  The beers coming out of the East Coast are exciting and continually growing.  It will be great to follow along!

So head over to http://atlanticcanadabeerblog.wordpress.com/ follow his .rss (if you're geeky like me) and enjoy.

Cheers, Greg! Here's to the future.
