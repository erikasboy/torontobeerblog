I just came across this at Greg Clow's blog, Beer Beats and Bites.

Apparently the Granite brewery, in celebration of their 19th anniversary in Toronto, is offering a present for anybody else turning 19 this year.  Come in to the brewery (Eglington and Mount Pleasant) on your birthday (with some form of proof), and you will get your first pint, a granite-branded glass, and a growler of beer, free.  Yes, you read that correctly.  Think of all the soon-to-be 19 year-olds in your life, and do them a favour: start them off (legally) on the right foot, with some locally produced craft beer.

Cheers to Ron Keefe and the crew at the Granite.  Here's to the next 19 years.
