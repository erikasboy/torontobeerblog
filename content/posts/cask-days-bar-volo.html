ABOUT CASK DAYS

October has become Toronto’s annual month to celebrate cask-condition ale – a term for unfiltered, unpasteurized, naturally carbonated beer that undergoes secondary fermentation. Once tapped, cask-ale - also referred to as real ale, has only a few days to be consumed at it's peak; we call these days 'Cask Days'.

During this time of the year, Cask Days attracts brewers from Ontario and Quebec to showcase their finest brews. This event has encouraged brewers and fans of craft beer to experiment and explore diverse styles of beer. To commence the 6th annual Cask Days, we are happy to announce that this is the biggest yet – with cask events hosted across Ontario during the month of October.

Thanks to everyone that has supported Cask Days since the beginning; it's been a pleasure in making this event an annual tradition for the Ontario craft beer community.

Cheers!

Ralph Morana,
Founder of Cask Days
