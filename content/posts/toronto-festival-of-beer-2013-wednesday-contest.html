It's hump day, and what better way to get over the crest than to win yourself four tickets to Toronto's Festival of Beer this Sunday? Just the thought of you, Steam Whistle box hat on your head, beer in one hand, food in the other, listening to Big Sugar, should be enough to make the last few days of this week sail past.  So how could that be you? Simple, tell me what beer 5 Paddles Brewing says is  "a great or terrifying introduction to 5 Paddles Brewing depending on your perspective." All correct entries will be pooled and the winner will be drawn from them.

Please note, you will need to come to the east end of Toronto (or have a friend do so) to pick up the tickets.  Contest closes at midnight EST tonight (Wednesday, July 24th).
