For round three, we were joined by J.Love, who does interactive media for Corus (these videos are part of interactive, you see). He is a newly minted GTA resident, via Chicago, and is stoked to see such a vibrant brewing culture here in Southern Ontario. Being a frequent drinker in his native USA of the likes of Stone, Lagunitas, Founders, etc, he is at least as qualified as Fred and I to judge. This week we had a Dry Stout from Jeremy Coghill, and "Muddy Porter" from Jeff Manol (Hoptomology).  Two very well respected home brewers from the GTA, it's going to be a good one!
