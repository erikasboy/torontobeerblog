Just saw on Facebook, Volo has got some new bottles in, and coming in.

Bottles from The Meantime Brewery in London England are now in and being served, and bottles from The Garrison Brewing Co.  in Halifax Nova Scotia will be arriving shortly!

Don't forget Mondays are $5/pint nights, as well.  And the olive bowl is still one of the most sublimely wonderful late night snacks in Toronto.
