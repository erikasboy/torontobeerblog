So, as some of you will remember from an earlier post, this Saturday is the launch party of BBbarfly, at the Amsterdam brewery.  I can begin to say how excited I am for this party.  Aside from being at one of my favourite brewer's, it is launching a pretty cool product.  BBbarfly is a company that produces some of the coolest bottle openers I've seen in a while.  They are basically butterfly knives, with openers instead of blades.  Pretty slick.  Fully brandable, the potential for them is amazing.  Branded merch for bars or breweries, giveaways for corporate events, cool wedding-party presents, the list goes on.  Check out their facebook event page for details on how you can attend too. http://www.facebook.com/event.php?eid=162959713754370
