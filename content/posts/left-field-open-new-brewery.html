Hey, remember last spring when I shared news about a new brewery in the east end? Left Field was being started by husband and wife Mark and Mandie Murphy, and they would be contract brewing their amazing three beers, Eephus, Maris*, and 6-4-3 IIPA. Well, that post went sort of nutso.

Well, less than a year after opening, today Mark and Mandy announced that they have secured premises for their very own brewery.  They'll be working relentlessly through the year to restore a lot of the building's original beauty, and obviously will be building a brewery in there.... They're aiming to be open in early 2015, but the good news is, they're hosting a little brewery-warming party, on February 22nd.  Details are here: https://www.facebook.com/events/1453419941543683/

Full news is here: http://www.leftfieldbrewery.ca/news/were-building-a-brewery
