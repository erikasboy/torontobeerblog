Okay, I'm totally falling behind here.  My review bottles are gathering dust (or disappearing with no review), and I've been to way more events then I've mentioned here.  So, in an effort to try to get back on the horse, here's a great event to start out your December:

CASK Social! is hosting their last social of '10, this Saturday, December 5th, at the Victory Cafe, 581 Markham Street (south on the first street west of Honest Ed's), from 4-7pm.  If you haven't been to a social yet, you really ought to go.  They're always a great event, with excellent beer (this month it is from F&M), and a super friendly group of people who all love cask ales.  There is no admission cost, just turn up, ready to drink excellent beer and enjoy the company of like minded people.

Cheers to the whole CASK Social crew, for an excellent series of events.  Here's to many more.
