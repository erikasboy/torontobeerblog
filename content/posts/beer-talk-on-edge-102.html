So, I didn't want to mention it, as it was one of those things I was a little afraid wouldn't happen, but last Friday I was lucky enough to sit down with "Fearless" Fred Kennedy, the afternoon drive guy on Edge 102.1.  Not really knowing much about how interviews on radios and such go, I was a tad afraid I would get cut.  I showed up with a case of beer, which I hoped would help my cause.  It turned out I was the only person there, aside from Fred, and he brought me into the studio, and we got to it.  We chatted for about 8 minutes, drank some lovely Amsterdam Oranje Weisse, and generally had a nice time.  From what I hear (and what Fred told me), there was a minute long clip on the air, and Fred posted the full interview on his blog.  If you missed it, you can hear it at the link below.  Sounds like we're going to do it again, so I'll make sure I give some warning this time.
