I nearly missed this one.  Thanks to Ken for mentioning it when he dropped out kegs at Castro's!

For this month, our Cask! Social will be teaming up with the Toronto Beer Lovers Meetup Group

Admission is $15 per person which includes cask ale and simple snacks.  To respect the brewery's capacity, a maximum of 70 CASK! members will be admitted. Sign up for "save me a spot" if you'd like us to hold a spot for you at the social or, if you'd rather decide last-minute, feel free to drop by and we'll do our best to squeeze you in!

There will be a range of cask ales, including a special Calibration brew from Black Oak's pilot system.

TTC directions:


Location 75 Horner Avenue, Unit # 1
On the corner of Judson & Horner
Go To Islington Subway Station
Take 110 A Islington South
(or take 110 Islington South and get off at Judson and walk West until you get to Horner)

A note from Black Oak:
One cup per person
One name tag per person
Early people will be under-appreciated.
We discourage driving, but encouraging having a designated driver.

No Official tour but there will be lots of Black Oak People to answer questions!!!!

Event rsvp at http://blackoaksocial.eventbrite.com/. I'm going to try to make it, hope to see you all there!
