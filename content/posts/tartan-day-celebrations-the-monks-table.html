Ah, nothing beats a good tartan.  I own a kilt and look for just about any opportunity to don it.  So as if I wasn't excited enough about another series of events at The Monk's Table, one that includes tartans puts me over the edge. If you're not familiar with The Monk's Table, you should be.  One of my favourite bars in Toronto, at Yonge and Summerhill, The Monk's Table serves a huge list of European brews, both on tap and in bottle.  It seems to be the only bar in the city with a wholly European selection, they also have an incredible food menu, and excellent service.  One of their mainstays, is their frequent parties and celebrations, always featuring great beer and food.  Their Robbie Burns week events were a massive success, and now we can look forward to another week of events, in commemoration of Tartan Day.

If you haven't heard of Tartan Day, don't worry, it's a fairly recent addition to our celebratory calendar.  On October 22, 2010 the Honourable James Moore, Minister of Canadian Heritage declared April 6th and every April 6th there after as a Tartan Day in Canada. A celebration of all things Celtic, The Monk's Table willl be celebrating Celtic culture, and highlighting how people of Celtic traditions have impacted our growth as a Nation.  Needless to say, there will be much fun and celebration.
