I just came across this article, after reading a post over at Saint John's Wort.  If you don't know BrewDog, look into them.  I posted a note about them producing "Sink The Bismark" a while back, but they have upped the ante.  The promised to make a stronger beer, if England lost to Germany in the round of 16 at the World Cup, and name it after the winning Goal scorer.  Hence, look for a new World's Strongest Beer, carrying the name Podolski, after the man who scored the second goal in the game.
