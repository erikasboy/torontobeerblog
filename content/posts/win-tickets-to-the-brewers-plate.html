If you have been to The Brewer's Plate before, you probably hardly need an introduction and enticement. Just go ahead and scroll down to see how you can win a pair of tickets to this event.

If not, let me tell you about one of the very best, often (unfairly) over-looked, beer events in the city.

Brewer's Plate is a fantastic evening of beers paired with some of the best breweries from the GTA. It's a charitable event, in support of The Friends of the Greenbelt, who work to protect and support the largest permanently protect greenbelt (the natural, often agricultural area that surrounds/"belts" urban areas) in the world. If you live in the GTA and like fresh fruits and vegetables, clean air, and sparkling drinking water, you love the greenbelt, even if you didn't realize it.

[caption id="attachment_3242" align="aligncenter" width="621"] Food from 2012 — Photo from http://www.torontolife.com/galleries/brewers-plate/[/caption]

I've been fortunate to attend Brewer's Plate a few times, and it is always a stand-out event for me. Incredible brewers, including Amsteredam, Sawdust, Muskoka, Black Oak, and a whole heap more. Some of the best chefs in the city preparing food. Other specialities like ChocoSol and my friends over at Station Cold Brew. There's going to be a bloody local gin producer. That I have to miss this year's is a tragedy of Shakespearian proportion.

You can check out the full line up (and mourn with me) here: http://www.brewersplatetoronto.org

But you, my friend, and blog-reader of immensely great taste, do not have to miss it. The event goes down this Wednesday, May 13th, at Corus Quay (25 Dockside Dr) starting at 6pm. Tickets are $95, which would be worth it for the food and drink alone, but when you consider that this is a fund-raiser for a fantastic cause, that price seems surprisingly low. That said, I feel you, if dropping over two bills (including TTC to the event, DO NOT DRIVE, trust me) is out of your range.
