I know that hair provides a good layer of insulation, but I'm always pleasantly reminded of that fact whenever I cut my hair short. And last night I cut my hair *really* short. AND today in Toronto it's cool and breezy, so my lid is being tickled by gentle whispers of cool air. The only thing better feeling, is the sense that I've helped raise fund to support St Baldrick's, who in turn support research to cure children's cancer and also programs to provide support and care for kids with cancer, and survivors.

[caption id="" align="aligncenter" width="450"] Action shot![/caption]

Last night at Sick Kids Hospital, 45 people shaved their heads, and so far as a group, we've raised about $36 000 for St Baldrick's, but the fund-raising continues! You can still make donations at my profile page here: http://www.stbaldricks.org/participants/ChrisSchryer

Thanks so much for all the support, whether it was a donation, a share or RT, or just kind words, I've been so impressed and blessed by all the good vibes you guys have sent my way.

And a big congratulations to Russ B. the winner of yesterday's bottle of Amsterdam Double Tempest Imperial Stout!
