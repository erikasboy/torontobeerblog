New year's resolution? No not even close. When I tuned into the live feed of Times Square at 11:59:38 and watched 22 seconds of ball dropping, starting a beer blog was the last thing on my mind. So why are we all here?
Well, from time to time, I like to post on facebook what beer I'm drinking, or what event I'm attending. But I find their status update limitations, well, limiting. So the logical step was to start a blog. How very 2002 of me...... Also I found out there is an app for my iPhone (I'm using it right now) that allows me to manage the blog away from my home, so that pretty much sealed the deal.

So what can we all look forward to (or "why you should come back")?

Well, I drink a lot of beer, and like to talk about it. So I'm sure we can expect a good deal of that. I also visit numerous bars around the city (Toronto, in case you missed the logo/URL). I'm sure some of the posts will be review type things about these bars. I also frequently find myself at events, and truly enjoy bragging about them. Lastly, I host regular beer events at Castro's Lounge, and so at least some posts will be blatant self-marketing. And I'm sure I will find more ways to enjoy the sound of my fingers typing.

So thanks for coming by, I hope you enjoy the site.
